# Code of Conduct

Be decent to each other.

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements here are about file formats, not people.
- No harassment, personal attacks, or hostility — in issues, pull requests, or
  commit messages.
- Criticise the code, not the person who wrote it.

Maintainers may edit, lock, or delete anything that ignores the above, and may
block repeat offenders.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer (@coldensjo) privately on GitHub.
Reports are handled in confidence.
